# 📊 Customer Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This end-to-end Data Analytics project analyzes customer purchasing behaviour, subscription trends, product performance, customer demographics, and revenue patterns. The project combines *Python, SQL, and Power BI* to clean, analyze, and visualize customer data while generating actionable business insights and recommendations.

---

# 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python (Pandas)
- SQL (MySQL)
- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- DAX

---

# 🎯 Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze customer purchasing behaviour, identify high-performing and low-performing product categories, understand customer segments, and provide data-driven business recommendations to improve sales and customer retention.

---

# 🚀 Key Insights

- Total Customers: *3.9K*
- Total Revenue: *$233.08K*
- Clothing generated the highest revenue (*$104K*).
- Clothing and Accessories contributed approximately *76%* of the total revenue.
- Young Adults are the largest customer segment (*1,028 customers*).
- Only *27%* of customers have active subscriptions.
- Outerwear generated the lowest revenue (*$19K*).

---

# 📌 Business Recommendations

- Launch attractive membership offers to increase the *27% subscription rate*.
- Increase inventory and promotional campaigns for Clothing and Accessories.
- Introduce discounts and bundle offers to improve Outerwear sales.
- Design targeted marketing campaigns for Young Adults.
- Improve customer satisfaction to increase the average review rating above *4.0*.
